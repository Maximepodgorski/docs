## What Lyse does

Lyse is an AI assistant that captures work where it happens. Connect Slack and your issue tracker, then mention `@Lyse` in any thread to generate a task from the conversation.

- Bugs, feature requests, decisions, action items—extracted automatically
- Full context from the conversation included in the task
- Tasks land directly in Linear, GitHub, Jira, or GitLab

![Hero Dark](/images/hero-dark.png)

## Who it's for

Product teams who lose context in Slack threads.

**Product managers** capture decisions from discussions. Instead of copy-pasting to Jira, mention `@Lyse` and the task is created in seconds.

**Developers** get tasks with full context. They know what to do and why.

**Anyone on the team** can mention `@Lyse` to turn a conversation into action.

## How it connects

```
Slack thread → @Lyse → Task created
```

1. A conversation happens in Slack
2. Someone mentions `@Lyse`
3. AI extracts the context and creates a task
4. The task appears in your issue tracker

## Add design context

Share a Figma link in your message and Lyse includes component details in the task—name, properties, and a direct link back to Figma.
