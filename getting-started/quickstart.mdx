## Prerequisites

- A Slack workspace where you can install apps
- One of these issue trackers: Linear, GitHub, Jira, or GitLab

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account" titleSize="h3">
    Go to [<u>app.getlyse.com</u>](http://app.getlyse.com) and sign up with Google or Magic Link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Slack" titleSize="h3">
    1. Go to **Settings \> Integrations**
    2. Click **Connect Slack**
    3. Select your workspace and authorize Lyse

    <Info>
      Once connected, `@Lyse` is available in your workspace.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your task tracker" titleSize="h3">
    1. In [**Integrations**](https://app.getlyse.com/integrations), find your issue tracker
    2. Click **Connect** and complete the authorization
    3. Select your default team or project

    <Info>
      Lyse supports **Linear**, **GitHub** Issues, **Jira**, and **GitLab** Issues.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first task" titleSize="h3">
    1. Find a Slack thread with some discussion
    2. Reply with `@Lyse` or `@Lyse create a bug task`
    3. Check your issue tracker—the task is there
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add design context

Include a Figma link in your message and Lyse extracts component details automatically.

1. Go to **Settings \> Integrations \> Figma**
2. Click **Connect Figma** and authorize
3. Now share Figma links with `@Lyse` to include design context in tasks

## Done

From now on, mention `@Lyse` in any Slack thread to create tasks with full context.
