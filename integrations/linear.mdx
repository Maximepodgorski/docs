## Setup

1. Go to **Settings \> Integrations \> Linear**
2. Click **Connect** and authorize Lyse
3. Select your default team and project
4. Click **Save**

## What gets created

When you mention `@Lyse` in Slack, a Linear issue is created with:

- Title generated from the conversation
- Description with full context
- Assigned to your default team and project

## Configuration

Change your default team and project anytime in **Settings \> Integrations \> Linear**.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Connection failed">
    Make sure you have permission to install apps in your Linear workspace. Try disconnecting and reconnecting.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Tasks not appearing">
    - Check your Linear connection is active
    - Verify the selected team and project still exist
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
